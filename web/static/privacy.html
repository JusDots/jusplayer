Privacy Policy

JusPlayer · last updated 7 November 2026

The short version: JusPlayer has no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. Your sign-in stays on the server that serves the app, and downloads you make stay on your own device.

What we collect

  • Nothing, unless you sign in. Browsing, searching and playing work without an account and no personal data is recorded.
  • If you sign in with Google, the gateway keeps a signed session (your YouTube Music cookies and an OAuth token) in a secure, HTTP-only cookie so it can ask YouTube Music for your account, liked songs, library and personalised feeds.
  • If you sign in with a YouTube cookie, that cookie is stored the same way, in the same signed session.

What we do not do

  • No analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no usage telemetry.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we have nothing to sell because we collect no personal data in the first place.
  • No advertising and no third-party ad network.

Where your data lives

The web app talks to a gateway that runs on Vercel. The session cookie is signed with a secret that never leaves the server, expires after 30 days and is deleted when you sign out. The desktop app and the terminal client keep their session in their own configuration file on your computer, and never send it anywhere else.

Downloads and local files

Tracks you download are written to your own device (your browser's storage, or a folder you choose in the desktop app). Files you import from your disk stay there; JusPlayer only reads them to play them.

Third parties

When you sign in, Google authenticates you and YouTube Music serves the catalogue and the audio. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic. If you use the Discord integration, your listening status is shared with Discord, and only if you configure it.

Your choices

Sign out at any time to delete the session. Clear your browser's cookies, or delete the session entry in the client configuration, to remove it completely. Because we keep no copy of your data anywhere else, that is all it takes.
